Masar — Saudi Job Market Pipeline

Capstone · SDA × WeCloudData Data Engineering Bootcamp · Team of 3
Live

Job ads in Saudi Arabia are spread across many websites. Masar collects them every day from three job sites, cleans them, and shows them in one place: a job board for job seekers and a dashboard for market trends.
